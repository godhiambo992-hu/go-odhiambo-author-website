A NOTE FROM THE AUTHOR

About G.O. Odhiambo

G.O. Odhiambo is a writer interested in the tensions that shape human life: relationships, identity, belief, ambition, memory and the consequences of choice. His fiction brings together reflective storytelling, compelling characters and questions that remain long after the final page.

This website is the home of his novels, literary projects and conversations with readers.
