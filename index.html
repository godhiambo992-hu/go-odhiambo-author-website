ABOUT THE AUTHOR

G.O. Odhiambo

G.O. Odhiambo is a writer interested in the human stories behind public events and private decisions. His work explores relationships, society, faith, ambition, injustice and the complicated spaces between what people say and what they truly feel.

This is the official home of his novels, reading excerpts and conversations with readers.
